## 2. What Bills is, and what it is not

Bills helps a group of people work out who owes what after shopping together. You photograph a
receipt or type it in, say who each item was for, and it does the arithmetic.

**Bills does not move money.** It is not a bank, a payment service, a wallet, or a way of
transferring anything. No money passes through it, and it has no connection to your bank, card or
any payment app. Settling up is something you and your friends do yourselves, however you
normally would.

**Bills is not financial, tax or accounting advice**, and its output is not a receipt, an invoice
or a record for any official purpose.

## 3. The bit that actually matters: check the numbers

Reading a receipt from a photograph is done by an AI model, and **it will sometimes be wrong**.
Creased paper, faded ink, an unusual layout, a smudged decimal point.

**You are responsible for checking what the app read before relying on it.** That is why every
item, price and total is editable, why the app tells you when the items don't add up to the
total, and why it never hides a figure it is unsure about.

The arithmetic Bills does on the numbers you confirm is exact, to the cent. What it cannot promise
is that the numbers it read off the paper are the numbers actually printed on it.

If a scan is wrong and you split a bill on it without checking, that is not something I can be
responsible for. Type it in by hand if it matters — that has no limit and never will.

## 4. Free use, and what costs money

Splitting bills is free and unlimited. Adding receipts by hand is free and unlimited. There is no
limit on trips, on people, or on anything the app calculates.

**Scanning photographs costs money to provide**, so it is limited. In the current version the
limit depends on how the app is set up:

- **The web app**: you supply your own Anthropic API key and pay Anthropic directly for what you
  use. Bills takes nothing, and I never see the key.
