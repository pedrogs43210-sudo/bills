## The short version

Bills keeps your trips, your friends' names, your receipts and who owes whom **on your own
phone**. There is no account, and nobody — including me — can see any of it.

The one thing that leaves your phone is a **photo of a receipt**, when you choose to scan one. It
goes to Anthropic to be read, and is not stored afterwards.

## What stays on your device and never leaves it

All of the following is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone:

- Trips, their names and dates
- The names of the people you split with, and the groups you make
- Receipts: shop names, dates, items, quantities, prices, who paid, who owes whom
- Your settings, including your own API key if you use the web version with one

None of this is transmitted anywhere, is not backed up to any server, and cannot be seen by me.
It is deleted when you delete the app or clear its data. **Because of this, there is no way for
me to recover it for you** — Settings → Export is the only backup, and it saves a file wherever
you choose.

## What leaves your device, and why

### Receipt photos

When you tap Scan receipt:

1. The photo is shrunk on your phone to at most 1568 pixels on its longest edge.
2. It is sent to **Anthropic** (`api.anthropic.com`), which reads the text and returns the list of
   items, quantities, prices and totals.
3. The app shows you that list to check.

The photo is **not stored** by Bills — not on any server, not in any log. In the phone apps it
passes through a small server of mine (on Cloudflare) purely to add the API key and forward it;
that server writes nothing about the photo to disk.

Anthropic processes the photo as a service provider. What they do with it is governed by their
own terms: <https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy> and
<https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms>.

**Legal basis (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)):** performing the service you asked for. You choose when a
photo is sent; nothing is sent if you type receipts in by hand.

### A count of your free scans

The phone apps ask a server of mine how many free scans this copy of the app has used, so the
monthly allowance means something. That server stores:

| Stored | Why |
|---|---|
| A random reference for your install (a UUID) | To count scans against something |
| The current month, and how many scans were used | The allowance itself |
| The time of the last scan | To stop a script hammering the service |

**That reference is not your name, your email, your phone or your device's identity.** It is a
random number generated by the app on first launch, and it is not connected to anything else
about you. It is shown to you in Help &amp; about, so you can quote it if you want the record
deleted.

Reinstalling the app produces a new reference and forgets the old one.

**Legal basis (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)):** a legitimate interest in not having a free service abused
into unlimited cost.

## What Bills does *not* do

- **No accounts.** No email address, no password, no sign-up, ever.
- **No analytics.** Nothing measures what you tap, which screens you visit, or how often you open
  the app.
- **No crash reporting** at present.
- **No advertising** at present, and no advertising identifiers.
- **No tracking across apps or websites**, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone for
  marketing.
- **No location, contacts, calendar, microphone or health data.** The app never asks for them.
- The camera is used only when you tap Scan receipt, and only for that photo.

## Children

Bills is not aimed at children and collects nothing that would identify one. It also has nothing
in it a child could spend money on.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and
complain to a supervisory authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

In practice, for this app:

- **The data that matters is on your phone**, so you already have complete access to it, can
  change any of it, and can delete all of it. Nobody needs to ask me.
- **The scan-count record** is the only thing I hold. Quote the install reference from Help &amp;
  about and I will delete it. I have no way to find it from your name or email, because I have
  never had either.
- **A receipt photo** is not retained by me at all, so there is nothing of it to hand over or
  erase.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what happens to your information, the date at the
top will change and the app will point you at it. Material changes to what leaves your phone will
be said plainly, not buried.
